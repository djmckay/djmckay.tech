Privacy Policy

DJ McKay built the Countdown With Me app. This app offers free and optional paid (Pro) features.

This page explains what information Countdown With Me collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information You Provide

Countdown With Me lets you create countdowns to events you choose. The information you enter — event names, dates, photos, colors, and locations — is stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, and syncs across your devices. This data is not visible to or collected by me; it's stored under your Apple ID, subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

If you use the Discover tab to browse curated events (holidays, movie releases, etc.), that catalog is read from a public database and does not require or collect any personal information.

Permissions the App May Request

  • Calendar — to let you create countdowns from your existing calendar events.
  • Contacts — to let you import birthdays as recurring countdowns. Contact data is read on-device only and is not transmitted anywhere except as part of the countdown you choose to create.
  • Location (optional, when adding a place to a countdown) — used to show a weather forecast for your event; processed via Apple Weather services.
  • Photos — to let you set countdown background images and build a keepsake photo scrapbook. Photos you add sync via your private iCloud account like other countdown data.
  • Notifications — to remind you about upcoming events and to power Live Activities / Dynamic Island countdowns.

None of these permissions are required to use the core app — each only unlocks the specific feature it's tied to, and you can decline any of them.

Advertising

Free-tier use of the app shows ads served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may collect device and usage information to serve and measure ads; see Google's Privacy Policy for details. Purchasing the Pro upgrade removes ads entirely.

In-App Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple — I never see or store your payment details.

On-Device AI Features (iOS 26+)

On supported devices, the app can generate countdown themes and artwork using Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence (FoundationModels and Image Playground). This processing happens on your device; no countdown data is sent to me or to Apple's servers for this feature.

Log Data

If the app encounters an error, standard crash/diagnostic information (device type, OS version, app version, and the circumstances of the error) may be collected to help fix bugs. This does not include your countdown content.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact me so it can be removed.

Data Deletion

Deleting a countdown in the app removes it from your iCloud account. Deleting the app and its iCloud data removes everything the app has stored.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; changes take effect as soon as they're posted on this page.
